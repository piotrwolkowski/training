# Dumbbell fly

Substitute for `cable-fly` when no cable crossover or pec deck is available. Flat or low incline
bench, elbow angle fixed throughout.

`failure_allowed: false`, unlike the cable and machine versions it replaces. The resistance
curve is the reason: a dumbbell fly is hardest at the bottom, with the shoulder in a loaded
stretch, so a set taken to true failure fails in the least safe position. Cables and the pec
deck load the squeeze instead, which is why they can be pushed to failure and this cannot. Stop
at RIR 1 and take the session's failure work on lateral raises and rear delts.

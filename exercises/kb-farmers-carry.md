# Kettlebell farmer's carry

Prescribed in distance or time, not reps. Heavy grip demand - do not pair with rowing.

With mismatched bells this becomes an **uneven carry**: different loads in each hand, which adds a
real anti-lateral-flexion demand on the trunk. Legitimate and useful, but swap hands each round
and log it as uneven, because the two sides are not doing the same work.

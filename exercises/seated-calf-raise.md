# Seated calf raise

Knee bent, pad across the thighs. Biases the soleus, where the standing version biases the
gastrocnemius - so the two are complements rather than substitutes.

Load sits on the thighs rather than the shoulders, so it places no demand on the upper back or
traps at all. That makes it the calf movement to use when the shoulder girdle needs to be left
alone - a standing calf machine puts pads directly on the traps, and holding dumbbells loads
them too.

Pause in the bottom stretch. Failure permitted.

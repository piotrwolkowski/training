# EZ bar curl

loads_elbow - never to failure. The EZ bar's angled grip is semi-supinated rather than fully
supinated, so it demands less forearm rotation than a straight bar. Where an athlete profile
restricts supination, `hammer-curl` is the more conservative choice.

# Chest-supported incline rear delt fly

Face-down on a bench set to roughly 30 degrees, light dumbbells hanging. Raise the arms out to
the sides until level with the shoulders.

Listed as a substitute, but frequently the better choice outright: chest support removes all
lower-back demand, which matters on any day following heavy hinging. It also makes cheating
nearly impossible - there is no torso to swing - so the rear delt actually does the work.

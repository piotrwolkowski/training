# Single-leg standing calf raise

One foot on a step, heel hanging, a single dumbbell or kettlebell in the hand on the working
side. Free hand on a rack for balance only. Reps per leg.

Solves the ceiling problem in held-weight calf work. Standing on two legs with a bell in each
hand caps out at whatever the hands can hold, and grip fails long before the calves do. On one
leg, bodyweight is not halved but the held load is - so per-calf load goes up sharply while grip
demand goes down.

Also avoids the traps entirely, unlike a shoulder-pad standing calf machine. Pause in the bottom
stretch. Failure permitted.

# Single-arm dumbbell row

One knee and one hand braced on a bench, rowing a single dumbbell. Reps per arm.

Substitute for `chest-supported-row` when the machine is unavailable, but not an equivalent one.
Chest support removes lower-back demand entirely, which is what makes that movement legal to
pair with squats. Bracing on a bench reduces the demand without removing it, and grip demand is
higher because the load hangs from one hand. On a day that already includes squatting and
hanging work, that stacking matters.

# Assault bike, Tabata

Eight rounds of 20 s hard / 10 s easy - 3 min 50 s of work. Performed as a buy-in at the start
of a session: warm-up and conditioning in the same four minutes.

Scored in **calories**, which makes it a genuine performance instrument rather than just a warm-
up. Because the score is sensitive to fatigue, sleep and readiness, it doubles as an objective
readiness signal - more reliable than a subjective 1-5 rating, and free. Track a personal
baseline; a sharp drop against it is information.

Not counted toward hard-set volume. Recorded with `calories` and `duration_s`; `rir` is not
meaningful here and is omitted.

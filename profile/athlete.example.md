# Athlete profile

> Copy this to `athlete.md` and fill it in. That file is gitignored.

## Goals

What you are training for, stated plainly. Strength and mass are largely the same programme at
intermediate training age; they differ mainly in rep-range distribution.

## Training age

Years of **consistent, progressive** lifting — not calendar years since you first touched a
barbell. This decides how much periodisation you need and how fast you can progress.

## Baselines

| Lift | Performance | Estimated 1RM | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell bench press | 60 kg × 6 @ RIR 2 | ~72 kg | Measured, not estimated. |
| Back squat | untested | — | Establish by RIR in week 1. |

Record what you actually did, with the RIR. Working loads are set by RIR, not by percentage of a
max — see the glossary. Testing a true 1RM is optional at best and buys nothing the programme
needs.

## Availability

Sessions per week and minutes per session. Note whether the budget is **fragmented** (many short
sessions) or concentrated — it changes the whole design. See `constraints.example.md`.

## Concurrent training

Any other sport or activity. It counts toward training load whether or not it is part of the
lifting plan, and its demands — grip, systemic fatigue, joint load — have to be scheduled around.

## Recovery context

Sleep, stress, anything that reliably affects how you train. A stall with poor sleep behind it
needs a different response than a stall without.
